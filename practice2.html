Avengers: Doomsday

Avengers: Doomsday is one of the movies I'm most excited about this year. It's the first big Avengers team-up film in a long time, and it's set to bring together heroes from a few different corners of the Marvel universe for one huge story.

The movie is directed by Anthony and Joe Russo, the same duo behind Avengers: Endgame, and the cast mixes classic Avengers with characters from the Fantastic Four and even some old X-Men movies. You can read more about the full cast and production on its Wikipedia page

What Has Me Most Curious

The biggest twist so far is that Robert Downey Jr. is returning to the franchise, but not as Iron Man this time. He's playing the villain, Doctor Doom, which raises a lot of questions about how the story will connect to everything that happened in the earlier movies.
